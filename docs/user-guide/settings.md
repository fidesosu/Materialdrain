# Settings

The gear at the top right of the screen. Each category is its own page.

## Account

Your Pixeldrain sign-in: a username or e-mail and password (with a two-factor code when your account has one), a login
link sent by e-mail, or an API key. See [Signing in to Pixeldrain](index.md#signing-in-to-pixeldrain). The sign-ins of
other hosts are on their own cards, under **Advanced**.

## Filesystem

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Hide .search_index.gz** | Pixeldrain keeps the paths of your files in this file, and makes it again when it's deleted. Hidden by default, so it can't be deleted by accident |

## Uploads

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Upload in the order the files were changed** | Several files go up oldest first, by when they were last changed on the device, one after another. On a host which lists files by upload date, they then appear in that order, newest at the top. Off, up to three go up at once, in the order they were picked |

## Search

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Most results shown** | All of them, or at most 200, 50 or 20. Searching a big folder tree can match thousands of files; a limit can help on slower phones |

## Notifications

Links to Android's own settings for the app's three kinds of notification: transfer progress, finished transfers and
failed transfers. Each can have its own importance, sound and lock screen visibility there.

## Previews

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Blurred backdrop** | A file's thumbnail, blurred, fills the space around a full-screen image and shows while a preview loads |
| **Wrap long lines** | Long lines of a text preview break onto the next line; off, they keep their length and scroll sideways |

## Accessibility

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Reduce animations** | Leaves out sliding, scaling, spinning and rolling numbers, and scrolls without animation; screens only fade. Also on when animations are turned off in Android's own settings |

## Security

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Lock with fingerprint or face** | Asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock when the app opens, and again after it's been in the background for more than 30 seconds |

## Advanced

Your hosts: each one's card (sign-in, connection test, updates, edit, export, remove), and **Add a custom host**. See
[Hosts](../hosts/index.md).

## Developer

Things being tried out. **FAB navigation prototype** replaces the bottom tabs with a floating button: tap it for a
window of the screens, swipe it sideways to move it to the left, middle or right. **Preview navigation as** shows that
window with a much bigger (pretend) menu, to see how it copes.
