    Materialdrain¶

    Materialdrain is an Android app for your files on Pixeldrain and on other hosts: a Nextcloud or other WebDAV server, an S3 bucket, a Windows or NAS SMB share, or any REST API you describe in a small JSON file. Browse, upload, download, preview, search, share and organise them, all from one app, in Material 3.

    What it can do¶
    Hosts Pixeldrain (built in), WebDAV, S3, SMB, and any REST API, side by side. Switch between them at the top of the screen
    Browsing A folder browser, a flat list of all your files, and Pixeldrain's lists, each sortable by name, size or date
    Search Searches the open folder and every folder under it, with results as you type
    Uploads Several files at once, from the app or shared from any other app, optionally in the order they were last changed
    Downloads Into your Downloads folder, with progress, cancelling, and opening the file when it's done
    Previews Images (HEIC and AVIF too), video and audio with seeking, text and code with colours, and the contents of archives
    Thumbnails Photos, video frames and album covers, even from hosts that make none themselves
    Background Transfers keep going with the app closed, with a notification to follow and cancel them
    Privacy Sign-ins are encrypted on the device, never part of a config, and the app can be locked with your fingerprint
